FAQ

Customization Frequently Asked Questions

Can I completely design my own basket from scratch?
Absolutely! Our fully bespoke service lets you select every item, choose your packaging, pick your ribbon color, and write your personal message. Simply use our contact form or speak with our gifting specialists to start building your custom basket.
Is there a minimum order for customized baskets?
There's no minimum for personal customized orders — a single bespoke basket is welcome! For corporate and bulk customized orders with logo branding, we recommend a minimum of 10 units for most branding options.
How much notice do I need to give for custom orders?
We recommend 3–5 business days for personal custom baskets, and 2–4 weeks for large corporate customized orders. Rush orders may be available — please contact us to discuss.
Can you accommodate multiple dietary restrictions in one basket?
Yes! We can curate baskets that are simultaneously vegan, gluten-free, and nut-free where needed. Just share all dietary requirements with our team and we'll ensure every selected item complies.
