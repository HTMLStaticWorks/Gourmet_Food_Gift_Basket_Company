Choose & Customize Your Basket

Ordering your perfect gourmet gift basket is easy. Just follow these steps to create a thoughtful, personalized gift.

STEP 1

Browse our collections by occasion — birthday, wedding, holiday, corporate event, or a heartfelt thank-you. Select the one that best fits your gifting need.

Customize your basket contents — choose your gourmet items, add dietary preferences (vegan, gluten-free, nut-free), and personalize with a message card.
