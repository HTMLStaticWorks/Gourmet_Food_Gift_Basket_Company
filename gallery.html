How It Works

Your Corporate Gifting Programme

From brief to delivery, our dedicated corporate team handles every detail of your gifting campaign.

STEP 1

Brief & Brand Discovery

Share your brand identity, gifting budget, and recipient profile with your dedicated account manager. We listen, advise, and propose a curated selection tailored to your corporate values.

STEP 2

Custom Curation & Branding

We curate your basket contents and apply your corporate branding — logo packaging, branded ribbons, custom inserts, and personalized notes — ensuring every basket is unmistakably yours.

STEP 3

Bulk Assembly & Delivery

We assemble and dispatch your entire order on schedule — whether 10 or 500 baskets — to a single address or multiple recipients across the country with full tracking provided.
