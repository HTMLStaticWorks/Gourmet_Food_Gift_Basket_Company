Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you fulfil an order?
Standard orders ship within 2–3 business days. Custom baskets require 3–5 business days, and corporate bulk orders need 2–4 weeks depending on volume and branding.
Do you ship nationally?
Yes! We ship across all 50 US states with temperature-controlled packaging to ensure freshness. International shipping is available for select markets — please enquire directly.
What is the minimum for bulk orders?
Our bulk pricing starts at 10 baskets. For corporate branding programs, we recommend a minimum of 10 units. There's no minimum for standard personal gift orders.
