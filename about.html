Who We Are

About The Gifted Pantry

Learn about our passion for gourmet artisan foods, our gifting philosophy, and our unwavering commitment to making every basket a beautiful, lasting memory.

Founded in 2014

A Decade of Curating Gifting Joy

The Gifted Pantry was founded by food lover and entrepreneur Claire Ashworth, who believed that the best gift you could give someone was the joy of discovering extraordinary artisan food. After years of scouring farmers markets, specialty food halls, and gourmet importers, Claire began building the curated collections that would become The Gifted Pantry — a company dedicated to making artisan gourmet gifting effortless and beautiful.

Artisan First

We work exclusively with small-batch, artisan producers who share our passion for quality and craft.

Beautifully Packaged

Every basket is a work of art — thoughtfully arranged, elegantly wrapped, and ready to impress.

Inclusively Curated

From vegan to gluten-free to kosher, we ensure everyone receives a delicious, appropriate gift.

Our Standards

Quality, Provenance & Presentation

Every product in every basket meets our rigorous sourcing standards — from farm to presentation, we never compromise.

Artisan Provenance

Every cheese, chocolate, and specialty food is sourced from certified artisan producers with traceable, ethical supply chains.

Premium Packaging

All baskets are assembled with FSC-certified packaging materials, premium satin ribbons, and food-safe insulating liners for freshness.

Sustainable Choices

We prioritize eco-conscious producers, organic certifications, and sustainable packaging across our entire range of gourmet gifts.

Safe & Compliant

Full allergen transparency, clear dietary labeling, and food-grade certified packaging for every single basket we produce.

Our Gifting Philosophy

Thoughtful Curation, Joyful Discovery

We believe that the best gift baskets don't just contain good food — they tell a story, spark discovery, and invite the recipient to slow down and savor something extraordinary. That's why we spend so much time seeking out truly special artisan producers: the small dairy making exceptional aged cheddars, the chocolatier using rare heritage cacao beans, the beekeeper harvesting single-floral raw honey.

When our customers send a basket, they're not just giving food — they're giving an experience, a moment of delight, and a connection to the passionate makers behind every product.
